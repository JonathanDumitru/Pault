In ProStatusManager.swift:

1. At the TOP of `refreshStatus()` (line 64), before any StoreKit logic, add:

   if ProcessInfo.processInfo.arguments.contains("--screenshot-mode") {
       isProUnlocked = true

3. Use `#if DEBUG` to ensure the override cannot exist in release builds, even though launch arguments cannot be injected into App Store builds. This is defensive.

4. Do NOT add a separate method or modify PaultApp.swift -- the override is entirely self-contained in refreshStatus().
