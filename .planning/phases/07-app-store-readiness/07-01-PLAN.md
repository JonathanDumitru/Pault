Verify: `spctl -a -v "$EXPORT_PATH/Pault.app"`. Package: `mkdir -p dist && "$SCRIPT_DIR/create_dmg.sh" "$EXPORT_PATH/Pault.app" "dist/Pault-${VERSION}.dmg" "Pault"`, then `xcrun stapler staple "dist/Pault-${VERSION}.dmg"`. Print success with DMG path.
